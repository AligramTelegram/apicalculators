ℹ What is a Merchant of Record?

A Merchant of Record (MoR) is the legal entity that processes payment and handles all tax obligations globally. Paddle and Lemon Squeezy collect VAT/sales tax in 100+ countries automatically. Stripe requires you to use Stripe Tax ($0.50 per transaction) or handle tax yourself.

💡 Break-even point

Paddle's 5% beats Stripe at MRR above ~$15K when adding Stripe Tax ($0.50/tx) and accounting for international card fees (1.5% extra). Below $15K MRR: Stripe is cheaper. Above $15K MRR with global customers: Paddle or Lemon Squeezy saves tax headaches.

Pricing Methodology

How we calculate payment processing fees.

Sources: All processing rates are sourced from each provider's official pricing pages — Stripe's pricing page, PayPal's merchant fees page, Paddle's pricing page, and Lemon Squeezy's pricing page. These are standard, public rates available to any developer without volume negotiation.

What the calculator includes: The base percentage rate and fixed per-transaction fee for standard card transactions. For Stripe, we show the base domestic rate (2.9% + $0.30). International card fees (+1.5%), currency conversion fees (+1%), Stripe Tax ($0.50/tx), and dispute/chargeback fees are not included in the automated calculation but are explained in the tips section.

What the calculator excludes: Stripe Radar fraud protection fees, ACH/bank transfer rates (typically 0.8%), Buy Now Pay Later surcharges (Klarna, Afterpay integrations), and subscription management platform fees (Stripe Billing adds a 0.5–0.8% fee on revenue managed through their platform).

Update cadence: Payment processor fees change infrequently — typically once every 1–2 years. We verify rates quarterly and update immediately when a provider announces a fee change.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Stripe cheaper than Paddle for SaaS?+

At the raw rate level, yes — 2.9% vs 5%. But adding Stripe Tax ($0.50/tx for EU VAT), international card fees (1.5% extra), and chargeback processing costs, the effective break-even is around $12–18K MRR depending on your customer geography. Below that Stripe is cheaper; above that Paddle's all-inclusive pricing is often comparable or better when you value tax compliance and no surprises.

What is a Merchant of Record?+

A Merchant of Record (MoR) is the legal entity that processes payment and assumes all tax obligations globally. Paddle and Lemon Squeezy are MoRs — they collect VAT/sales tax in 100+ countries, remit it to local tax authorities, and handle disputes. Stripe is not a MoR: you are the merchant of record, making you responsible for global tax compliance, which is complex and potentially costly for international businesses.

What is the difference between Lemon Squeezy and Paddle?+

Both are Merchant of Record platforms charging 5% + $0.50. Lemon Squeezy launched in 2021 with a developer-first UX, strong product page builder, and simple API. Paddle has been operating since 2012 with more enterprise features, a larger customer base, and more complex integration options. For indie developers and small SaaS, Lemon Squeezy is often easier to set up. For established companies with volume, Paddle offers more customization.

Does Stripe work globally?+

Stripe is available in 47+ countries for businesses to register in, and accepts payments from customers in 195+ countries. International card payments (non-domestic) incur a 1.5% surcharge. If your business is registered in a country Stripe supports but you sell to customers globally, you'll also need Stripe Tax or a separate VAT registration strategy for EU/UK compliance. Paddle and Lemon Squeezy handle this automatically.

What is PayPal's actual fee for SaaS?+

PayPal's standard rate for digital goods is 3.49% + $0.49 per transaction. There's also a 1.50% fee for international transactions. For subscription SaaS, PayPal's recurring billing options are more limited than Stripe. Most SaaS companies use PayPal as a supplementary checkout option (some customers strongly prefer it) rather than their primary processor, because Stripe's developer experience and subscription tooling is significantly better.

How do chargebacks affect payment processing costs?+

Chargebacks (disputed transactions) cost $15–25 per dispute with Stripe and PayPal, regardless of outcome. If your dispute rate exceeds 1% of transactions, Stripe may place your account under review. Paddle and Lemon Squeezy absorb chargeback risk as the Merchant of Record, so disputes don't directly affect your account standing. For products with higher refund rates (digital goods with unclear value propositions), MoR platforms provide meaningful risk protection.
