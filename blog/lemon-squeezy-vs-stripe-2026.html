Lemon Squeezy vs Stripe 2026: Which Is Cheaper for Indie Developers?

June 2, 2026 · 9 min read · By APICalculators

Lemon Squeezy's 5% looks 72% more expensive than Stripe's 2.9%. But for a solo developer selling internationally, Stripe's hidden costs — VAT compliance, Stripe Tax, chargeback fees, dunning tools — often flip the equation. Here's the real math.

The One-Line Answer

Under ~$8K MRR with international customers: Lemon Squeezy is usually cheaper on total cost. Over $8K MRR with a finance setup: Stripe wins. The break-even depends on your international revenue percentage and how much you value zero-ops tax compliance.

✦ The real break-even

The break-even is roughly $8K–$12K MRR for a typical indie SaaS with international customers. Below that: Lemon Squeezy's total cost is comparable or lower, with dramatically less operational overhead. Above that: migrate to Stripe.

Developer Experience

Lemon Squeezy wins on:

  • Setup speed: 30-minute integration with webhooks and a clean API
  • Zero tax ops: Never think about VAT, GST, or sales tax again
  • Checkout quality: Modern, conversion-optimized checkout UI out of the box
  • License keys: Built-in software licensing system — unique in the market
  • Customer portal: Self-service subscription management included

Stripe wins on:

  • Customization: Stripe Elements, custom checkout flows, embedded payments
  • API depth: The most comprehensive payment API in the industry
  • Scale: Volume discounts, enterprise contracts, dedicated support at high MRR
  • Connect: Marketplace/platform payments with split flows
  • International reach: More payment methods than any other processor
ℹ Lemon Squeezy vs Paddle

Both are Merchants of Record with identical 5% + $0.50 fees. Lemon Squeezy is better for indie developers and simple digital products. Paddle has more mature subscription billing, better enterprise features, and longer track record. For a solo founder's first SaaS: Lemon Squeezy. For a funded company: Paddle.

FAQ

Is Lemon Squeezy cheaper than Stripe?

On total cost at under $8K MRR with international customers, often yes. Lemon Squeezy's 5% includes VAT handling, chargebacks, and compliance. Stripe's 2.9% + Stripe Tax ($0.50/tx) + chargeback fees ($15 each) adds up faster than it looks.

Does Lemon Squeezy handle VAT?

Yes — as Merchant of Record, Lemon Squeezy collects and remits all VAT, sales tax, and GST globally. You never register for EU VAT or Australian GST. This is the core value proposition of the 5% fee.

What is the difference between Lemon Squeezy and Paddle?

Same MoR model, same fee (5% + $0.50). Lemon Squeezy is simpler, more indie-focused, has a more modern dashboard. Paddle has better enterprise subscription management and a longer track record at scale. Both are excellent options.
