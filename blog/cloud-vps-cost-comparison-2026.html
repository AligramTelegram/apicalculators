Cloud VPS Cost Comparison 2026: Hetzner vs Vultr vs DigitalOcean

2026-06-03 · 12 min read · APICalculators

Picking the wrong VPS provider costs developers hundreds per year. This guide compares Hetzner, Vultr, DigitalOcean and Linode on actual 2026 list prices so you can choose the cheapest option that meets your spec.

Why Cloud VPS Costs Vary So Much

Providers charge differently for identical specs because of data-center location, network egress pricing, and included extras like backups. A 4 GB / 2 vCPU instance ranges from $4.50/mo (Hetzner EU) to $24/mo (DO General Purpose). The gap is real money at scale.

Vultr and DigitalOcean: Best for Global Reach

Vultr has 32 locations including Seoul, São Paulo and Mumbai. DigitalOcean offers Managed Databases, App Platform and $200 free credits for new accounts. If you need a single VPS close to US users, both are solid at $6/mo for 1 GB.

Total Cost of Ownership: Don't Forget Egress

Hetzner includes 20 TB / mo free egress. DigitalOcean charges $0.01/GB after 1 TB free. At 10 TB/mo egress, Hetzner is effectively free vs $90/mo on DO. Run the numbers on your traffic before committing.

Total Cost of Ownership: Egress Is the Hidden Multiplier

Raw compute prices are only half the story. Network egress — data transferred out to the internet — is where providers make back margin lost on cheap VMs. Run these numbers before you sign a contract.

A media-serving app at 10 TB/mo egress pays €0 on Hetzner but up to $90/mo on DigitalOcean — on top of compute. At that scale Hetzner's effective all-in cost for a 4 GB instance is ~€8.50/mo vs $72/mo on DO. That's an 8× gap.

Watch out: inbound traffic (uploads, API calls to your server) is free on all major providers. The egress meter only ticks when data leaves your VPS toward users or the public internet.

FAQ

Is Hetzner cheaper than DigitalOcean?

Yes — often 3–5× cheaper for equivalent EU specs. Hetzner's CX22 (2 vCPU / 4 GB) is €4.50/mo vs DigitalOcean's $24/mo for the same spec. The trade-off is fewer global locations.

Which VPS is best for a small SaaS?

Hetzner for EU audience (cheapest + GDPR). Vultr for global reach (32 locations). DigitalOcean if you need managed Postgres or Redis alongside compute.

Choosing a Cloud VPS in 2026: Beyond the Price Tag

VPS pricing tables make it look like a commodity decision, but real-world performance, network quality, support responsiveness, and the total cost of adjacent services vary significantly between providers. Here is what to evaluate beyond the sticker price.

Network bandwidth and egress costs. Most VPS providers advertise generous included bandwidth (1-4TB/month), but overage charges vary wildly. Hetzner charges about 1.19 euros/TB overage; DigitalOcean charges $0.01/GB ($10/TB); AWS EC2 charges $0.09/GB ($90/TB). If your application serves substantial media or has high API traffic, egress can dwarf your compute cost. Always calculate your bandwidth budget before selecting a provider.

CPU performance consistency. Advertised vCPUs differ significantly in underlying hardware and sharing policies. Hetzner shared vCPUs on AMD EPYC deliver consistently strong benchmarks for the price. Budget providers on older Intel hardware with aggressive oversubscription can underperform by 2-4x on CPU-intensive workloads. Run sysbench or UnixBench on a trial instance before committing to an annual plan.

Managed service ecosystem. A $12/month VPS from DigitalOcean pairs with a $15/month Managed PostgreSQL database, $5/month managed Redis, and native Load Balancers — giving you a full stack for roughly $44/month with most operational burden handled. Building the same on a bare Hetzner VPS requires more DevOps investment but can cost 60-70% less. Choose based on your team operational capacity, not just the compute price.
