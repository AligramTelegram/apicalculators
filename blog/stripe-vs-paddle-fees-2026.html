Stripe vs Paddle vs Lemon Squeezy: True Cost for SaaS in 2026

June 2, 2026 · 11 min read · By APICalculators

Stripe looks cheaper at 2.9% vs Paddle's 5%. But once you add Stripe Tax, chargeback fees, EU VAT compliance costs, and international card fees, the math often flips. This guide shows the true total cost at $5K, $25K, and $100K MRR — so you choose based on real numbers, not headline rates.

The Key Difference: Merchant of Record

Before comparing fees, understand this: Stripe and Paddle are fundamentally different products.

Stripe is a payment processor. YOU are the merchant. You collect money, you handle tax, you fight chargebacks, you maintain PCI compliance, you register for VAT in 27+ countries.

Paddle and Lemon Squeezy are Merchants of Record (MoR). THEY are the legal merchant. They collect money on your behalf, handle all global tax compliance, absorb chargebacks, and manage subscriptions — in exchange for a higher cut.

ℹ The MoR equation

A SaaS with $25K MRR selling to 20+ countries would spend $300-800/month on Stripe Tax + $100-400/month on a tax compliance tool + developer time on failed payment recovery. That $1,000+/month in hidden costs can make Paddle's 5% look cheap at $1,250/month total vs Stripe's 2.9% + extras = $2,000+.

Hidden Costs Most Comparisons Miss

1. International card fees

Stripe charges an additional 1.5% for international cards (non-US cards on a US account). If 30% of your customers are international, your effective rate on those transactions is 4.4% + $0.30 — approaching Paddle's rate.

2. Stripe Tax ($0.50 per transaction)

If you're selling to the EU, UK, or Australia, you legally must collect VAT/GST. Stripe Tax automates this at $0.50/transaction — adding $40 on 80 transactions/month or $800 on 1,600 transactions/month.

3. Failed payment recovery

Stripe's dunning (failed payment retry) requires a third-party tool like Churnbuster ($50-150/month) or Stripe's own Smart Retries. Paddle includes this in their MoR service.

4. Chargeback disputes

Stripe charges $15 per dispute whether you win or lose. With a 0.3% chargeback rate on $100K MRR and 1,600 transactions, that's 5 disputes × $15 = $75/month in dispute fees before any refunds. Paddle absorbs this entirely.

When to Choose Each Processor

Choose Stripe if:

  • MRR above $15K and you have a finance/DevOps team to handle tax
  • US-only or very low international revenue (<10%)
  • You need maximum customization (Stripe Elements, Checkout, Billing)
  • You're building a marketplace or platform (Stripe Connect)
  • You already have tax compliance infrastructure — and want to calculate your full SaaS infrastructure cost

Choose Paddle or Lemon Squeezy if:

  • Early-stage SaaS (under $15K MRR) — simpler and often cheaper
  • Significant international revenue (EU, APAC) requiring VAT/GST
  • Solo founder or small team — offload all compliance overhead
  • You want chargebacks fully managed
  • Product-led growth with self-serve subscriptions

Paddle vs Lemon Squeezy:

Same fee structure, different audience. Paddle is better for established SaaS with complex subscription logic and volume discounts. Lemon Squeezy is better for indie developers, digital products, and teams that value simplicity and fast setup.

FAQ

Is Stripe cheaper than Paddle for SaaS?

At the raw fee level, yes. But once you add Stripe Tax ($0.50/tx), international card fees (+1.5%), and chargeback costs, the break-even is around $12-18K MRR. Below that, Paddle's all-in 5% is frequently cheaper on total cost.

What is a Merchant of Record?

A Merchant of Record (MoR) is the legal entity that processes the payment and handles all tax obligations. Paddle and Lemon Squeezy are MoRs — they collect VAT/sales tax globally and remit it for you. Stripe is not — you remain the legal seller and must handle tax compliance yourself.

Does Lemon Squeezy charge VAT?

No — as MoR, Lemon Squeezy handles all VAT, sales tax, and GST collection and remittance. Your 5% + $0.50 covers this entirely.
