Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026.

Calculator inputs

All calculators on this site run entirely in your browser using client-side JavaScript. Numbers you enter — token counts, user counts, revenue figures — are never transmitted to our servers or any third party. They exist only in your browser session and are lost on refresh, unless a specific tool states otherwise.

Theme preference

We store a single value (light or dark theme) in your browser's local storage to remember your preference. This is not shared or transmitted anywhere.

Analytics

We may use privacy-respecting, aggregate analytics to understand which calculators are used, without tracking individuals across sites or collecting personal data.
