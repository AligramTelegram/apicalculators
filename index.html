Stripe vs Paddle Fees — SaaS Payment Processor Cost Calculator

This payment processor fee calculator shows exactly how much Stripe, Paddle, Lemon Squeezy, and PayPal take from your SaaS revenue each month. For US-based SaaS companies, choosing the right payment processor can save tens of thousands of dollars annually. Stripe charges 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction and requires you to handle sales tax collection yourself (or pay for Stripe Tax). Paddle and Lemon Squeezy act as Merchant of Record — their 5–9% fee includes global VAT/sales tax compliance, EU VAT, and chargeback coverage. A US startup doing $25,000/month in SaaS revenue with 800 transactions would pay roughly $1,010/month with Stripe versus $1,250–$2,250 with Paddle, depending on their tier. But Paddle saves potentially $200–$500/month in tax compliance tooling. Enter your revenue and transaction count to see the real Stripe vs Paddle fee comparison for your business.

What are Stripe fees for SaaS subscriptions in 2026?

Stripe's standard US rate is 2.9% + $0.30 per successful card transaction. For subscriptions, Stripe Billing adds 0.5–0.8% on top. International cards add 1.5%. At $25,000/month revenue with 800 transactions, total Stripe fees land around $970–$1,050/month USD before tax tooling costs.

Is Paddle cheaper than Stripe for global SaaS?

Paddle's higher percentage (5–9%) looks expensive, but it includes global sales tax, VAT, EU digital services tax, and chargeback handling — all of which Stripe charges separately or requires third-party tools for. For US SaaS companies selling internationally, Paddle's total cost of compliance often matches or beats Stripe's true all-in cost.

Which payment processor is best for indie SaaS developers?

Lemon Squeezy is popular with US indie developers for its simplicity — it's Merchant of Record like Paddle but with a cleaner API and dashboard. For high-volume ($100K+/month) US B2B SaaS, Stripe is typically cheaper. For global consumer SaaS under $50K/month, Paddle or Lemon Squeezy reduces compliance overhead significantly.

AWS Lambda Cost Calculator — Serverless Pricing Comparison

This AWS Lambda cost calculator estimates your monthly serverless compute spend based on invocation count, average duration, and memory allocation. For US-based teams running event-driven architectures, Lambda's pricing model — $0.20 per 1M requests plus $0.0000166667 per GB-second — rewards low-memory, fast-executing functions. A typical US API backend making 1 million Lambda invocations per month at 150ms and 256 MB would pay roughly $5–8/month, well within Lambda's generous free tier of 1M requests/month. Heavy workloads exceeding 10M invocations at 1 GB memory can climb to $100–$200/month. Vercel Functions and Cloudflare Workers offer cheaper per-invocation pricing ($0.50/1M) but have execution time limits. This serverless pricing comparison helps you decide whether Lambda, Vercel, or Cloudflare Workers fits your cost and latency requirements.

How much does AWS Lambda cost for 1 million requests per month?

AWS Lambda's first 1M requests per month are free (permanent free tier). After that, it's $0.20 per 1M requests plus compute cost. For 1M invocations at 150ms and 256 MB, total monthly cost (including compute) is roughly $0.45 USD beyond the free tier. Lambda's cost rises significantly with longer execution times or higher memory.

Is Cloudflare Workers cheaper than AWS Lambda?

For most workloads, yes. Cloudflare Workers costs $0.50/1M requests on the paid plan (Workers Paid at $5/month base), with a 10ms CPU time limit per request. Lambda charges $0.20/1M requests but measures wall-clock time, not CPU time. For fast edge functions under 10ms CPU, Workers is cheaper. For longer-running compute, Lambda wins on flexibility.

How do I reduce AWS Lambda costs in production?

Use ARM/Graviton2 processors (20% cheaper than x86), right-size memory (more memory = faster = cheaper GB-seconds), enable provisioned concurrency only during peak hours, and implement request batching for SQS/Kinesis triggers. Use Lambda Power Tuning tool to find the memory sweet spot. This calculator lets you model memory changes before deploying them.

AI Agent Cost Calculator — Multi-Model Pipeline Cost

This AI agent cost calculator estimates the total cost of running multi-step LLM pipelines where different models handle different tasks. How much does a multi-step AI agent pipeline cost per run?

What is the cheapest LLM for AI agent routing and planning?
